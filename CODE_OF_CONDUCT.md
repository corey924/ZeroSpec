# Code of Conduct

> Traditional Chinese reference: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-TW.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-TW.md)

ZeroSpec is a practical, tool-agnostic project. We expect discussions and contributions to stay respectful, specific, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, direct, and concrete.
- Critique ideas, wording, examples, and changesets, not people.
- Assume good intent first, then address impact clearly when something causes harm or confusion.
- Keep reviews, issues, and discussions on topic and evidence-based.
- Make room for contributors with different experience levels, backgrounds, and tool choices.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, sexualized language, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, repeated bad-faith arguments, or pile-ons.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic disruption.
- Tool tribalism or dismissive behavior that blocks collaboration.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and other public interactions in this repository.

## Maintainer Actions

Maintainers may take any action needed to protect the project and its contributors, including:

- Requesting edits or clarification.
- Removing comments, content, or examples.
- Locking conversations.
- Temporarily or permanently restricting participation.

## Reporting

Do not use a public issue for personal conduct reports.

If you need to report a conduct problem, contact the repository maintainers privately through the contact method available on the maintainer GitHub profile. Share links, screenshots, and timestamps when possible.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and prioritize the safety of affected people.
